### Priority fixes
#### P0 — Several pages are missing a meta description
**Why it matters:** This is the little summary line people see on Google. Blank means Google writes its own — often the wrong sentence — so fewer people click through to you.

**How to fix:** Add a single clear sentence (under 160 characters) describing each page in your site editor's SEO/description field.

#### P1 — Your business phone number isn't shown the same way on every page
**Why it matters:** Google trusts a business more when its name, address and phone match everywhere. Inconsistency can hold back your local ranking.

**How to fix:** Pick one format for your phone number and use it identically in the footer of every page.

#### P2 — Some images have no alt text
**Why it matters:** Alt text helps Google understand your photos and helps visually impaired visitors. It's a small, safe win.

**How to fix:** Add a short description to each image in your editor's image settings.

### Quick wins
- Write meta descriptions for the 3 pages that are missing one.
- Standardise the phone number in the site footer.
- Add LocalBusiness schema so Google shows your hours and address.
