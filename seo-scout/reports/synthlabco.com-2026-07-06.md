### Priority fixes
#### P0 — No phone number or business address appears anywhere on the site
**Why it matters:** When people (and Google) can't find a phone number or location, they trust the business less and Google is less likely to show you to nearby customers searching for your services.

**How to fix:** Add your phone number and your city and state to the footer of every page and to the contact page, and make sure they match exactly what's on your Google Business Profile.

#### P1 — Location details are missing from the site's behind-the-scenes business information
**Why it matters:** Google reads a hidden 'business card' on your site to learn who you are and where you operate. Right now that card says what you do but not where, so you're at a disadvantage for searches like 'web design near me' or 'automation company in Indiana.'

**How to fix:** Have your web person add your address (or at least city, state, and service area) and phone number to the site's structured business information, matching your Google Business Profile exactly.

#### P1 — Pages load a bit slower than they should (speed score 78 out of 100)
**Why it matters:** Visitors on phones give up on slow pages, and Google favors faster sites. Every second of delay costs you potential inquiries.

**How to fix:** Ask your web person to shrink image file sizes and delay loading anything that isn't visible right away when the page opens. Aim for a speed score of 90 or better.

#### P2 — The site never mentions the towns or areas you serve in the main page text
**Why it matters:** If your service area isn't written on the page, Google has little reason to show you to people searching in those places. 'Indiana' only appears in a short description, not in the content people actually read.

**How to fix:** Add a short section to the homepage or about page naming the areas you serve, and consider a simple page for your main city describing what you do for businesses there.

#### P2 — The site doesn't link out to any trusted outside sources or profiles
**Why it matters:** Linking to your own social profiles, directory listings, or reviews helps visitors verify you're a real, established business and connects your site to your reputation elsewhere.

**How to fix:** Add footer links to your Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, or any directories and review sites where you're listed.

### Quick wins
- Put your phone number and city/state in the footer of every page
- Add your service area and location to the site's hidden business information so it matches your Google Business Profile
- Shrink oversized images to speed up page loading
- Add a sentence or two on the homepage naming the areas you serve
- Link to your Google Business Profile and social pages from the footer
